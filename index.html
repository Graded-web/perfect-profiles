The standard

The same photograph, every time.

Light
The lighting is measured and rebuilt the same way at every office we visit, so a photo taken at HQ matches one taken at a branch three time zones out.
Pace
About a minute a person, back to back. The busiest day we've run was 500 people.
Retouch
Every frame gets the same color grade, the same crop and a light retouch. Someone looks over the whole batch before it goes out.
Delivery
Files land within 48 hours of the last shot, already sized for LinkedIn, Slack and your internal directory.

How it works

Nobody has to leave the building.

  1. 01

    Booking

    Once we've agreed headcount, offices and dates, we send round a link and people book their own slot. You don't have to keep a spreadsheet.

  2. 02

    Shoot day

    The studio goes up in a meeting room, usually inside half an hour. People come down one at a time and are back at their desks a couple of minutes later.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    We pick the best frame for each person, run the batch through one edit, and send it over inside two days. Everyone gets a full-resolution master.
