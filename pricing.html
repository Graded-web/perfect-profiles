Pricing

Simple, scalable pricing for teams of any size.

There's no fixed price list — every engagement gets quoted against your actual team size and locations. Here's what drives the number.

Team size

Larger headcounts bring the per-person cost down — a 500-person shoot day costs less per employee than a 50-person one.

Number of locations

Each additional office adds photographer travel and setup time. We quote multi-location engagements as one coordinated package, not separate bookings.

Turnaround speed

Our standard delivery is 48 hours. Need it faster for an all-hands or a launch? Rush turnaround is available for an added fee.

What's included, at every price point

No add-on fees for the basics.

  • Photographer travel and full studio setup
  • Self-serve employee scheduling link
  • Retouching and color-matching on every photo
  • LinkedIn, Slack, and directory-sized exports
  • One full-resolution master per employee
  • A single point of contact for the whole engagement
FAQ

Common questions

How is the price actually calculated?

We quote a flat day rate per location based on your headcount, plus travel for any offices outside our base cities. You'll get one number for the whole engagement before you commit — no per-person surprises afterward.

Do you offer a minimum or maximum team size?

We've shot teams from 15 people to 600+ in a single engagement. Below about 15, a day rate usually doesn't make sense — we'll tell you upfront if that's the case for your team.

What if some employees are remote or miss the shoot day?

We can schedule a short make-up session on a follow-up visit, or point remote employees to a vetted local photographer using our same retouching specs, so the final result still matches.

Can we get a faster turnaround than 48 hours?

Yes — rush delivery (24 hours, or same-day for smaller teams) is available for an added fee. Mention your deadline when you request a quote.

Do you require a deposit?

We'll cover deposit terms when we send your quote — it varies by engagement size. Nothing is charged before you've approved a quote in writing.
