Pricing

Priced by the day, not the person.

There is no fixed price list — every engagement is quoted against your actual team size and locations. Three things drive the number.

  1. № 01

    Team size

    Larger headcounts bring the per-person cost down — a 500-person shoot day costs less per employee than a 50-person one.

  2. № 02

    Locations

    Each additional office adds photographer travel and setup time. Multi-location engagements are quoted as one coordinated package, not separate bookings.

  3. № 03

    Turnaround

    Standard delivery is 48 hours. Rush turnaround — for an all-hands or a launch — is available for an added fee.

Questions

Asked before every booking.

How is the price actually calculated?

We quote a flat day rate per location based on your headcount, plus travel for any offices outside our base cities. You receive one number for the whole engagement before you commit — no per-person surprises afterward.

Do you offer a minimum or maximum team size?

We've photographed teams from 15 people to 600+ in a single engagement. Below about 15, a day rate usually doesn't make sense — we'll tell you upfront if that's the case for your team.

What if some employees are remote or miss the shoot day?

We can schedule a short make-up session on a follow-up visit, or point remote employees to a vetted local photographer using our same retouching specs, so the final result still matches.

Can we get a faster turnaround than 48 hours?

Yes — rush delivery (24 hours, or same-day for smaller teams) is available for an added fee. Mention your deadline when you request a quote.

Do you require a deposit?

We'll cover deposit terms when we send your quote — it varies by engagement size. Nothing is charged before you've approved a quote in writing.
