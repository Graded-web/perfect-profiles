Pricing

Priced by the day, not the person.

There's no fixed price list. We quote against your actual team size and the offices we'd need to visit, and it mostly comes down to three things.

  1. 01

    Team size

    The bigger the headcount, the lower the cost per person. A 500-person shoot works out cheaper per employee than a 50-person one.

  2. 02

    Locations

    Every extra office adds travel and another setup. If you've got several, we quote the whole thing as one package rather than as separate bookings.

  3. 03

    Turnaround

    Standard delivery is 48 hours. If you're up against an all-hands or a launch we can go faster, for a bit more.

Questions

The ones we get asked most.

How is the price actually calculated?

It's a flat day rate per location, set by your headcount, plus travel for any offices outside our base cities. You get one number for the whole job up front, and nothing gets added per person afterwards.

Do you offer a minimum or maximum team size?

We've done teams of 15 and teams of 600+ in one go. Under about 15 people a day rate usually isn't worth it for you, and we'll say so rather than take the booking.

What if some employees are remote or miss the shoot day?

We can run a short make-up session on a follow-up visit. For people who are fully remote, we'll point them at a local photographer who works to our retouching specs so their photo still matches the rest.

Can we get a faster turnaround than 48 hours?

Yes. Rush delivery is 24 hours, or same-day for smaller teams, for an added fee. Mention your deadline when you ask for a quote.

Do you require a deposit?

Deposit terms depend on the size of the job and we'll set them out in the quote. Nothing is charged until you've approved that quote in writing.
