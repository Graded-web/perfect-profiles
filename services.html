How a shoot day works.

From the first scheduling email to the moment retouched files reach your team's inbox — here is exactly what happens.

  1. № 01

    Pre-shoot logistics

    We confirm headcount, locations, and dates, then send a self-serve booking link so employees pick their own sixty-second slot. No spreadsheets, no chasing people down.

  2. № 02

    On-site setup

    Our photographer arrives early and sets up a full studio rig — key light, fill light, backdrop, reflector — in a meeting room or quiet corner. Typically ready within thirty minutes.

  3. № 03

    The shoot day

    Employees show up for their slot, we shoot a short burst of frames per person, and they are back at their desk in under a minute. We keep pace with the booking schedule so no one waits.

  4. № 04

    Retouching & review

    Every employee's best frame receives the same color grade, crop, and light retouch pass, then a human review to make sure nothing looks off before it ships.

  5. № 05

    Delivery

    Finished headshots land within 48 hours, sized for LinkedIn, Slack, and your employee directory, plus one full-resolution master per person.
