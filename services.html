How your shoot day works.

Professional headshots made easy for you and your team – from simple self-scheduling and an on-site studio setup to beautifully finished images delivered next day.

  1. 01

    Pre-shoot planning

    • We’ll confirm your headcount, location, date and visual direction
    • We provide individual booking and rescheduling links
  2. 02

    On-site studio setup

    • We create a professional studio at your location
    • Premium lighting, backdrop and reflectors included
    • Setup completed in approximately 30 minutes
  3. 03

    The shoot day

    • Each person attends at their scheduled time
    • Relaxed, professional direction
    • Natural, confident and on-brand portraits
  4. 04

    Image selection and retouching

    • Each person selects their preferred portrait
    • Professional colour grading and retouching
    • Every image individually reviewed for consistency
  5. 05

    Delivery

    • Finished headshots delivered within 24 hours
    • Full-resolution master image for every person
