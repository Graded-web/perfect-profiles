How a shoot day works.

Everything that happens between the first scheduling email and the finished files landing in your team's inboxes.

  1. 01

    Pre-shoot logistics

    We agree headcount, offices and dates, then send over a booking link. Your team picks their own slots, so you're not maintaining a spreadsheet or chasing stragglers by email.

  2. 02

    On-site setup

    The photographer arrives early and builds the studio in a meeting room or a quiet corner: key light, fill light, backdrop, reflector. Usually ready inside half an hour.

  3. 03

    The shoot day

    People turn up for their slot, we shoot a short burst of frames, and that's it. We stay on schedule so nobody ends up queuing outside the meeting room.

  4. 04

    Retouching & review

    We pick the best frame for each person and give the whole batch the same color grade, crop and light retouch. Then someone goes through them by eye before anything is sent.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Finished headshots land within 48 hours, already cropped for LinkedIn, Slack and your staff directory, plus a full-resolution master for each person.
