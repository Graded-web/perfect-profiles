How your shoot day works.

Professional headshots made easy for you and your team—from simple self-scheduling and an on-site studio setup to beautifully finished images delivered next day.

  1. 01

    Pre-shoot planning

    We confirm your headcount, location, preferred date and visual direction to ensure the photography reflects your brand from the outset. Each team member receives a dedicated booking link to select their own time slot and reschedule if needed—no spreadsheets to manage or follow-up emails to send.

  2. 02

    On-site studio setup

    Your photographer arrives early and transforms a meeting room or quiet office space into a professional portrait studio, complete with premium lighting, backdrop and reflectors. Setup is usually complete within 30 minutes.

  3. 03

    The shoot day

    Each person arrives at their scheduled time and is photographed with their personality, role and your brand in mind. We provide clear, relaxed direction to capture a natural, confident portrait while keeping the day running smoothly and on schedule.

  4. 04

    Image selection, retouching and review

    Your photographer helps each team member review their images and select a portrait they feel confident and happy with. Every selected image is then professionally colour-graded, carefully retouched and individually reviewed to ensure the entire collection is polished, cohesive and of the highest standard.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Your finished headshots are delivered within 24 hours. A full-resolution master image is supplied for every person.
