How a shoot day works.

From the first scheduling email to the moment retouched files hit your team's inbox — here's exactly what happens.

01

Pre-shoot logistics

We confirm headcount, locations, and dates, then send a self-serve booking link so employees pick their own 60-second slot. No spreadsheets, no chasing people down.

02

On-site setup

Our photographer arrives early and sets up a full studio rig — key light, fill light, backdrop, reflector — in a meeting room or quiet corner. Typically ready within 30 minutes.

03

Shoot day

Employees show up for their slot, we shoot a short burst of frames per person, and they're back at their desk in under a minute. We keep pace with the booking schedule so no one waits.

04

Retouching & QA

Every employee's best frame gets the same color grade, crop, and light retouch pass, then a human review to make sure nothing looks off before it ships.

05

Delivery

Finished headshots land within 48 hours, pre-sized for LinkedIn, Slack, and your employee directory, plus one full-resolution master per person.
