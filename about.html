Who we are

The people behind Perfect Profile.

Lucas Dawson

Co-Founder and Creative Director

For more than 25 years, renowned photographer, creative director and business owner Lucas Dawson has created distinctive imagery and visual content for some of Australia’s and the world’s most recognised brands.

His experience spans commercial photography, advertising, fashion, major events, corporate content and video production. Throughout his career, Lucas has worked with leading organisations and iconic brands including Emirates, Mercedes-Benz, Crown, Louis Vuitton, NAB and the Melbourne Fashion Festival.

As the founder of Lucas Dawson Photography and Melbourne Studios, Lucas has built an exceptional network of photographers, videographers and creative professionals. Together, they produce compelling campaigns, corporate photography, advertising imagery, social content and event coverage for clients across Australia and internationally.

At the heart of Lucas’s work is a genuine understanding of people and brands. His approach goes beyond creating polished images—he captures personality, builds connection and produces visual content that is authentic, memorable and commercially effective.

Alysia Gilligan

Co-Founder and Managing Director

Alysia Gilligan is an experienced project and business leader with an extensive background in project management, international events, incentive programs and the delivery of complex, multi-market initiatives.

Throughout her career, Alysia has worked with and led teams across the Middle East, Asia and Australia. She has managed major international projects and collaborated with highly experienced marketing, public relations, communications and branding professionals around the world.

This international experience has given Alysia a deep understanding of how strong brands are built, communicated and protected across different markets and audiences. She brings strategic thinking, meticulous organisation and a highly personal approach to every client engagement, ensuring each project is professionally managed from the initial conversation through to final delivery.

Alysia’s focus is on understanding what makes each client distinctive and translating that into a professional presence that feels consistent, credible and genuinely representative of who they are.

How it started

The partnership behind Perfect Profile.

Perfect Profile was founded when Lucas and Alysia brought together their complementary experience in photography, creative direction, branding, communications, business leadership and international project delivery.

They recognised that while many talented professionals and respected organisations do exceptional work, the way they present themselves does not always reflect the quality, personality or credibility behind their brand.

Together, Lucas and Alysia created Perfect Profile to close that gap.

Their combined expertise enables Perfect Profile to offer more than outstanding photography. Every project is approached strategically, considering the client’s brand, audience, values and broader business objectives. The result is a professional presence that looks cohesive, feels authentic and builds confidence before a conversation even begins.

What we do

About Perfect Profile.

Perfect Profile helps organisations, teams and individuals present themselves with clarity, consistency and authenticity.

Specialising in professional headshots, brand photography and profile management, Perfect Profile creates credible, on-brand imagery and professional profiles that strengthen recognition, authority, trust and connection.

From photographing an entire national workforce to creating executive portraits, personal branding imagery or a complete commercial image library, every project is carefully planned and professionally managed. The experience is designed to be seamless for both the organisation and the people being photographed, with clear direction, thoughtful preparation and consistently high-quality results.

In a world increasingly filled with AI-generated imagery, generic content and online noise, authentic representation has never been more valuable. A professional profile should do more than look polished—it should communicate personality, inspire confidence and reflect the reputation a person or organisation has worked hard to build.

Perfect Profile helps clients protect and strengthen that reputation through imagery that feels genuinely human, unmistakably on-brand and worthy of the work behind it.

The network

The team behind the image.

Behind Perfect Profile is a trusted national network of some of Australia’s leading creative talent.

The team includes highly experienced photographers, videographers, producers, stylists, hair and makeup artists, retouchers and creative professionals, carefully selected to meet the needs of each project. This depth of talent allows Perfect Profile to deliver a consistent, premium service across Australia while providing the right creative expertise for every client, location and brief.

Whether coordinating a single executive portrait or a large-scale, multi-location photography program, Perfect Profile brings together the people, planning and creative capability required to deliver exceptional results.

It is this combination of strategic leadership, creative excellence and genuine human connection that sets Perfect Profile apart.
