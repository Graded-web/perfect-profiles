About

A headshot day, done properly.

Why we started this

The 3% problem.

At most companies, only a small fraction of employees ever get a proper headshot — usually whoever happened to ask, or whoever's been there long enough to have one from an old all-hands photo booth. Everyone else is on LinkedIn with a cropped vacation photo, or nothing at all.

We built Shoot because fixing that shouldn't require booking a studio, scheduling a half-day off-site, or hiring a different photographer for every office. It should be: we show up, your team shows up for sixty seconds each, everyone leaves with a photo that looks like it belongs next to everyone else's.

What we believe

A few things we won't compromise on.

Consistency over flair

Every employee gets the same lighting, crop, and retouch — not whichever angle happened to look best that day. The directory matters more than any single photo.

Respect the schedule

Sixty seconds means sixty seconds. We don't hold up someone's calendar to get a better shot — we get a good shot fast and let them get back to work.

One point of contact

Whoever books us is talking to the same person from quote to delivery — not bounced between sales, scheduling, and a separate retouching vendor.
