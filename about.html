About

A headshot day, done properly.

Why we started this

Most people never get one.

At most companies the only people with a decent headshot are the ones who thought to ask for one, plus whoever was around for that all-hands photo booth four years ago. Everyone else is on LinkedIn with a cropped holiday photo, or a grey silhouette.

Fixing that shouldn't mean booking a studio, losing half a day, and finding a different photographer for every office. So we made it simpler: we turn up, your team gives us a minute each, and everyone walks away with a photo that sits properly next to everyone else's.

How we work

Three things we're stubborn about.

  1. 01

    Consistency first

    Everyone gets the same lighting, crop and retouch, even when a different angle might have flattered one person more. What matters is how the whole directory looks together.

  2. 02

    We keep to the clock

    If we've said a minute, it's a minute. We'd rather get a good shot quickly than hold up someone's morning chasing a slightly better one.

  3. 03

    One person to talk to

    Whoever books us deals with the same person from the first quote through to delivery, rather than being passed between sales, scheduling and a retouching vendor.
