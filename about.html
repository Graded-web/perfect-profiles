About

A headshot day, done properly.

Why we started this

The 3% problem.

At most companies, only a small fraction of employees ever get a proper headshot — usually whoever happened to ask, or whoever has been there long enough to have one from an old all-hands photo booth. Everyone else is on LinkedIn with a cropped vacation photo, or nothing at all.

We built Perfect Profiles because fixing that shouldn't require booking a studio, scheduling a half-day off-site, or hiring a different photographer for every office. It should be: we show up, your team shows up for sixty seconds each, and everyone leaves with a photo that looks like it belongs next to everyone else's.

What we believe

A few things we won't compromise on.

  1. № 01

    Consistency over flair

    Every employee gets the same lighting, crop, and retouch — not whichever angle happened to look best that day. The directory matters more than any single photo.

  2. № 02

    Respect the schedule

    Sixty seconds means sixty seconds. We don't hold up someone's calendar to get a better shot — we get a good shot fast and let them get back to work.

  3. № 03

    One point of contact

    Whoever books us is talking to the same person from quote to delivery — not bounced between sales, scheduling, and a separate retouching vendor.
